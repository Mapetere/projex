# NON_GOALS.md — Projex v1

> What Projex explicitly refuses to do. These are not "future features" — they are architectural boundaries.

## Projex Does NOT Judge

❌ **No productivity scoring**
Projex will never compute "how productive" you were. It observes activity patterns but attaches no value judgment.

❌ **No quality assessment**
Projex will never say a project is "good" or "bad." It reports what it observes, not whether you should be proud of it.

❌ **No comparisons**
Projex will never rank projects against each other or compare you to other developers.

---

## Projex Does NOT Coach

❌ **No recommendations**
Projex will never suggest "you should work on X more" or "consider finishing Y."

❌ **No goals or targets**
Projex does not track progress toward goals. It has no concept of what you "should" be doing.

❌ **No notifications or nudges**
Projex does not remind, prompt, or push. It observes when asked.

---

## Projex Does NOT Automate Decisions

❌ **No auto-publishing**
Projex will never automatically update your portfolio, resume, or any public-facing content without explicit human approval.

❌ **No silent actions**
Every inference is a draft. Every action requires confirmation.

❌ **No "smart" defaults that hide complexity**
If there's uncertainty, Projex shows it. It does not collapse ambiguity into false confidence.

---

## Projex Does NOT Expand Scope (for v1)

❌ **No non-GitHub sources**
v1 observes GitHub only. GitLab, Bitbucket, local repos, and other sources are out of scope.

❌ **No GUI**
v1 is CLI-only. A desktop app or web interface is not a v1 goal.

❌ **No cloud sync or accounts**
v1 runs locally. There is no Projex account, no cloud storage, no sync.

❌ **No AI/LLM integration**
v1 uses deterministic heuristics only. No GPT, no embeddings, no machine learning.

---

## Why These Boundaries Exist

These non-goals exist to prevent Projex from becoming:

1. **A productivity tracker** — Those optimize for metrics, not meaning
2. **A coach or manager** — Developers don't need another voice telling them what to do
3. **An automation that acts without consent** — Trust requires explicit approval
4. **A bloated multi-purpose tool** — Focus enables quality

Projex is an **observability probe**. It sees and reports. That's it.
